      ▌ A GA MAEBA
      audiovisual work · original M/V

      

        
          
          
▶ A GA MAEBA — M/V  ·  ↳ watch on YouTube

        
        

          An ambivalent emphasis on both the sonic and visual registers — the two pulling against each other only to converge, univocally, on the sense of urgency dictated by accelerationism and transhumanism. The track samples Kenji Kawai's Making of a Cyborg — the opening cue from Mamoru Oshii's Ghost in the Shell — folding it into a multimedial work that reaches the same charged territory mapped by hyperstition and Nick Land's cyber-schizo spiels; a synesthetic representation of an overwhelming sense of urgency towards a dance we're forcefully partaking in: an impelling symbiosis between man and machine.

      ▌ RESONANT BENDS · NEMA ENSEMBLE × mi·ɽài
      realtime audio-responsive visuals improvised with touchdesigner · live show 2025

      
      

        
          
          
▶ FULL PERFORMANCE  ·  ↳ YT playlist

        
        

          A concert-project conceived by NEMA Ensemble exploring timbre as a generative force of musical form. Drawing from French spectral thought (Grisey, Murail, Dufourt) and its roots in Debussy and Messiaen, it unfolds as a continuous perceptual journey — listening guided by evolving sonic qualities rather than themes or narratives.
          “What happens when musical form is allowed to emerge from the internal transformation of sound itself?”

            ▶ read full▼ collapse
            

              Resonant Bends is a concert-project conceived by NEMA Ensemble as an exploration of timbre as a generative force of musical form. Rather than presenting a strict "spectral program", the project traces a broader constellation of ideas, linking spectral music to its precursors and consequences across different repertoires, media, and listening modes.
              At the core of the project lies a simple yet radical question:
              “What happens when musical form is allowed to emerge from the internal transformation of sound itself?”
              Drawing inspiration from French spectral thought (Grisey, Murail, Dufourt) and its deep roots in Debussy and Messiaen, Resonant Bends unfolds as a continuous perceptual journey. Electronics, acoustic instruments, original compositions, commissions, and transcriptions coexist within a single dramaturgical arc, where listening is guided not by themes or narratives, but by evolving sonic qualities.
              The program moves from electronic thresholds to ritual gestures, from microscopic attention to sound to expansive harmonic spaces, finally returning to Debussy's Nuages — a timeless figure of suspended time and floating resonance.
              This playlist documents the full live performance at Spazio Arena (Avellino), capturing Resonant Bends as a unified listening experience rather than a sequence of isolated works.
